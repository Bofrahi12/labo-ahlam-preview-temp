Questions from practices

How do we start working together?

Simply send your first form via the form above (or a WhatsApp message): the lab reviews the case and replies to you directly. No prior commitment.

What are the production timelines?

The timeline is given as soon as each case is received, depending on the type of work — no generic timeline: you know what to expect before you start.

How are partner rates calculated?

Rates are worked out according to the type of work and your volume, and discussed directly with Ahlam — no generic public price applies to partner practices.

How do I send the impression and the case photos?

The case photos are sent on WhatsApp right after the form. For the physical impression, how it's sent is decided together based on your city, at first contact.
