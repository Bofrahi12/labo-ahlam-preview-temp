Night guard (retainer & bruxism) · Labo Ahlam — Tanger

Night guard in Tangier — after braces & against teeth grinding

A night guard is worn at night for two reasons: after orthodontic treatment it keeps the result and stops the teeth from drifting back, and for people who grind their teeth in their sleep it protects the teeth from wear. It is made here in Morocco, custom-fit to your mouth.

What makes this service special?

  • Keeps your orthodontic result — teeth don't drift back
  • Protects against wear from night grinding (bruxism)
  • Made in Morocco, exactly to the measure of your mouth
  • Lightweight — worn only at night

How does the service work?

01

Consultation

You write on WhatsApp or by private message, explain your case and what you want, and Ahlam replies with the suitable options.

02

Impression & model

You try the prosthesis and we adjust it until it feels comfortable and natural in your mouth — and follow-up stays open.
