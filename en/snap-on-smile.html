Snap-on Smile · Labo Ahlam — Tanger

Snap-on Smile in Tangier — a new smile, no drilling, no pain

Snap-on Smile is a removable smile that clips over your teeth and changes their shape instantly — no drilling and no pain. A quick solution for anyone who wants a new smile without touching their original teeth.

What makes this service special?

  • Removable — clips on and off over your teeth
  • No drilling, no pain
  • Changes the shape of your smile instantly
  • Real client testimonial on the main site — his case was ready in two weeks

How does the service work?

01

Consultation

You write on WhatsApp or by private message, explain your case and what you want, and Ahlam replies with the suitable options.

02

Impression & model

We take a precise impression of your mouth and make the model — the base on which the whole prosthesis is built.

03

Made in the lab

The prosthesis is handmade in the laboratory, in the material you chose.

04

Fitting & follow-up

You try the prosthesis and we adjust it until it feels comfortable and natural in your mouth — and follow-up stays open.

Questions about this service

Does Snap-on Smile require drilling the teeth?

No — it clips over your teeth as they are, with no drilling and no pain, and it's removable at any time.

How long does it take to be ready?

Timing varies by case — for example, one Snap-on Smile client's case was ready in two weeks. Your exact timeline is confirmed right at booking.

How do I book an appointment?

Simply: WhatsApp or a private message on Instagram. Working hours: 10:00–13:00 and 15:00–19:30. Send the service you want and you'll get the details back.

Are prices fixed?

The price is calculated per case, type and material — that's why Ahlam prefers to answer you directly once she knows your case, instead of general numbers that don't apply to everyone.

Want to know if Snap-on Smile is right for you?

The price is calculated for your case — send your question on WhatsApp and Ahlam will reply with the details.
