Classic stellite partial denture · Labo Ahlam — Tanger

Classic Stellite denture in Tangier — stability with a metal frame

The classic partial denture is the traditional choice with a metal framework (Stellite) for people who need extra stability. At the lab we explain the difference between flexible and classic so you can choose what suits your case.

What makes this service special?

  • Stellite metal framework for extra stability
  • The traditional choice, proven over years
  • At the lab, the difference with flexible is explained before any decision
  • Price per case and material

How does the service work?

01

Consultation

You write on WhatsApp or by private message, explain your case and what you want, and Ahlam replies with the suitable options.

02

Impression & model

We take a precise impression of your mouth and make the model — the base on which the whole prosthesis is built.

03

Made in the lab

The prosthesis is handmade in the laboratory, in the material you chose.

04

Fitting & follow-up

You try the prosthesis and we adjust it until it feels comfortable and natural in your mouth — and follow-up stays open.

Questions about this service

What's the difference between flexible and classic?

Flexible is light with no metal frame; classic has a metal framework that gives more stability. At the lab we explain the difference in detail so you can choose what suits your case.

Who is the classic one for?

People who need more stability in their denture — the exact choice is made after the consultation, once we know your case.

How do I book an appointment?

The price is calculated per case, type and material — that's why Ahlam prefers to answer you directly once she knows your case, instead of general numbers that don't apply to everyone.
