Ceramic crowns & veneers in Tangier

Ceramic crowns and veneers with a natural finish — the models are shown and explained to you in detail at the laboratory before any decision, so you can see the expected result with your own eyes and choose with confidence.

What makes this service special?

  • Ceramic with a natural-looking finish
  • Models are shown and explained to you in detail at the lab
  • No decision before you've seen and understood the expected result
  • Price depends on the number of teeth and the type — at the consultation

How does the service work?

01

Consultation

You write on WhatsApp or by private message, explain your case and what you want, and Ahlam replies with the suitable options.

02

Impression & model

We take a precise impression of your mouth and make the model — the base on which the whole prosthesis is built.

03

Made in the lab

The prosthesis is handmade in the laboratory, in the material you chose.

04

Fitting & follow-up

You try the prosthesis and we adjust it until it feels comfortable and natural in your mouth — and follow-up stays open.

Questions about this service

Will the result look natural?

The goal is a natural finish — that's why the models are shown and explained to you in detail at the lab before any decision, so you can see the expected result with your own eyes.

How do I choose between a crown and a veneer?

The difference is explained at the lab based on your tooth's condition and what you want to achieve — the decision comes after the explanation, not before.

How do I book an appointment?

Simply: WhatsApp or a private message on Instagram. Working hours: 10:00–13:00 and 15:00–19:30. Send the service you want and you'll get the details back.

Are prices fixed?

The price is calculated per case, type and material — that's why Ahlam prefers to answer you directly once she knows your case, instead of general numbers that don't apply to everyone.

Want to know if Ceramic crowns & veneers is right for you?

The price is calculated for your case — send your question on WhatsApp and Ahlam will reply with the details.
